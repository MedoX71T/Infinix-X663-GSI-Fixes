Problem Wall

Risks and build-specific reports.

See issue reports

Which firmware is supported?

Use your original firmware’s files. Other builds may not fit.

Does LK provide network support?

No. LK warning removal is optional; the boot patch serves the networking task.

Which images should I download?

Back up data and recovery partitions before unlocking.

Is every GSI feature supported?

No. VoLTE, VoWiFi, hotspot, and SELinux depend on firmware and GSI.
