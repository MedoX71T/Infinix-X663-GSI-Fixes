Which X663 firmware fits?

Use the listed stock build or your own original firmware images.

Does LK fix networking?

No. LK removes boot warnings. The boot patch handles networking.

Does every GSI feature work?

Carrier features, hotspot, and SELinux depend on your firmware and GSI.

What belongs in an X663 report?

Stock build, GSI release, changes, symptoms, results, and redacted logs.
