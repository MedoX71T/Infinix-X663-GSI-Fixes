# GSI-Ready X663 Brand Colors

## Primary Palette
- **Light Beige**: `#F5E6D3` - backgrounds, cards
- **Medium Beige**: `#D4C5B0` - secondary elements
- **Light Brown**: `#8B6F47` - primary text, borders
- **Dark Brown**: `#5D4E37` - headings, emphasis
- **Deep Brown**: `#3E2723` - backgrounds, contrast

## Accent Colors
- **Warm Orange**: `#D2691E` - CTAs, highlights
- **Success Green**: `#6B8E23` - success states
- **Error Red**: `#CD5C5C` - warnings

## Usage
- Background: `#F5E6D3` or `#3E2723` (dark mode)
- Primary: `#5D4E37`
- Accent: `#D2691E`
